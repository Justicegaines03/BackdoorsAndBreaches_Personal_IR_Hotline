# Business Challenge Deck Outline

## Slide 1 - Title
TSOC Personal Incident Response Pilot  
Training Student Analysts to Support University Individuals

## Slide 2 - The Gap
- Personal cyber incidents are increasing.
- Production SOC cannot absorb individual advisory demand.
- Need a scalable, low-risk support model.

## Slide 3 - Pilot Strategy
- Training-only pilot with clear boundaries.
- Backdoors and Breaches simulation as delivery engine.
- University-specific scenarios and escalation checkpoints.

## Slide 4 - Operating Model
- Intake analyst, triage lead, communications lead, incident commander.
- 60-minute session format with inject-driven decision stress.
- Referral-only outcomes aligned to existing university pathways.

## Slide 5 - What We Built
- Charter and governance boundaries.
- Custom TSOC personal-IR deck.
- Facilitator and learner workflow kit.
- Metrics collection and after-action process.

## Slide 6 - Pilot Evidence
- 5 cohorts completed.
- Faster triage and escalation decisions over time.
- Improved communication quality and process adherence.

## Slide 7 - Risk Management
- No live response in pilot.
- No sensitive PII in training notes.
- Supervisor-owned escalation model.
- Explicit differentiation from production SOC.

## Slide 8 - Value Proposition
- Improves student readiness for real SOC work.
- Expands cyber resilience support capacity.
- Creates repeatable process and measurable outcomes.

## Slide 9 - Next Phase
- Governance hardening and approved contact matrix.
- Guided support office hours under supervision.
- Competency gates before participant advancement.

## Slide 10 - Ask
- Approve continuation to guided-support readiness phase.
- Endorse cross-unit alignment for formal escalation pathways.
