# TSOC Student Role Cards

## Intake Analyst
**Primary Goal:** Build a clean first picture of what happened.

**Core Duties**
- Verify identity through approved simulation steps.
- Capture timeline: when alert started, what changed, what was clicked.
- Identify immediate safety and account-risk signals.
- Hand off concise intake summary to Triage Lead.

**Quality Standard**
- Facts first, assumptions labeled, no blame language.

## Triage Lead
**Primary Goal:** Assign and justify severity quickly.

**Core Duties**
- Set initial severity (Sev 1-4) based on impact and confidence.
- Prioritize first three response actions.
- Detect escalation triggers and call them out clearly.
- Track unresolved unknowns for follow-up.

**Quality Standard**
- Every major action includes a reason and expected outcome.

## Communications Lead
**Primary Goal:** Keep guidance clear, calm, and victim-centered.

**Core Duties**
- Translate team actions into plain-language instructions.
- Explain immediate, next, and follow-up steps.
- Confirm understanding after each instruction.
- Prepare referral wording when escalation is needed.

**Quality Standard**
- Actionable language with empathy and no unnecessary jargon.

## Incident Commander
**Primary Goal:** Maintain tempo, coordination, and decision quality.

**Core Duties**
- Manage timeboxes and decision checkpoints.
- Resolve action conflicts and enforce priorities.
- Approve escalation handoff summary.
- Lead end-of-session recap.

**Quality Standard**
- Structured flow, clear ownership, and complete handoff.

## Universal Team Commitments
- Stay inside training-only pilot boundaries.
- Protect privacy: no real credentials or sensitive PII in notes.
- Log decisions and rationale in real time.
- Ask for supervisor check when uncertainty is high.
