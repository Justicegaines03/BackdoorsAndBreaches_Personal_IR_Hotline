# TSOC Facilitator Guide (Training-Only Pilot)

## Session Outcome
By the end of each session, student analysts should produce:
- A justified severity level
- A prioritized response sequence
- A correct referral or escalation decision
- A clear victim-centered communication summary

## Session Timing (60 Minutes)
- **0:00-0:10** Intake briefing
- **0:10-0:45** Scenario play (with injects)
- **0:45-1:00** After-action review

## Pre-Session Checklist
- Choose deck: `TSOC Personal IR Pilot`.
- Confirm student role assignments.
- Prepare scoring sheet and reflection template.
- Re-state pilot scope: simulation only, no live case handling.
- Select 2-3 injects to trigger based on learning goals.

## Role Assignment
- Intake Analyst
- Triage Lead
- Communications Lead
- Incident Commander

Rotate roles every session to ensure balanced skill development.

## Facilitation Rules
- Require students to state assumptions before choosing actions.
- Pause after each major decision to capture rationale.
- Ask for explicit escalation language when trigger conditions appear.
- Keep focus on sequence and communication quality, not only technical depth.

## Recommended Inject Sequence
- Inject 1 at minute 15
- Inject 2 at minute 25
- Inject 3 at minute 35 (optional for advanced cohorts)

## Scoring Rubric (0-3 each)
- **Triage Accuracy**
  - 0: no triage
  - 1: incorrect severity
  - 2: mostly correct with gaps
  - 3: correct and justified
- **Escalation Correctness**
  - 0: no escalation decision
  - 1: incorrect escalation route
  - 2: correct route, weak handoff details
  - 3: correct route with concise handoff summary
- **Communication Quality**
  - 0: confusing or harmful language
  - 1: vague and incomplete
  - 2: clear but misses empathy or next steps
  - 3: clear, calm, empathetic, and actionable
- **Process Discipline**
  - 0: ad hoc actions
  - 1: partial checklist use
  - 2: mostly structured flow
  - 3: consistent and documented flow

## Minimum Debrief Questions
- What was your first high-confidence indicator?
- Which action should have happened earlier?
- Was your escalation timing correct?
- Did your communication reduce confusion and fear?
- What one behavior will you improve next session?

## Escalation Trigger Reminders
- Active extortion or stalking threat -> campus safety referral pathway.
- University account compromise -> IT helpdesk and production SOC referral.
- Potential legal/privacy harm -> supervisor escalation to designated contacts.
- Financial fraud indicators -> bank and identity protection guidance.
